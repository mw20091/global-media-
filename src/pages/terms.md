  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> October 25, 2026</p>

  <p>This Privacy Policy governs the data processing practices of TT Media ("we," "us," or "our"). We operate a privacy-first news platform designed to deliver editorial content without invasive data collection. This document outlines our commitment to data protection, transparency, and compliance with applicable privacy frameworks, including the GDPR and CCPA.</p>

  <h2>1. Information Collection and Scope</h2>

  <p><strong>Subscriber Data:</strong> We collect only your voluntarily provided email address when you subscribe to receive editorial updates or newsletters.</p>

  <p><strong>Infrastructure Logs:</strong> Our servers automatically log standard, non-identifying technical data, such as browser type, traffic volume, and device information, solely to maintain platform security, improve performance, and prevent denial-of-service activity.</p>

  <p><strong>Comments and Contact Forms:</strong> If you contact us or submit a message, we may retain the information you provide for responding to your inquiry, resolving issues, or handling editorial or advertising requests.</p>

  <h2>2. Legal Basis for Processing</h2>

  <p>We process personal data under the legal basis of consent where applicable, including when you subscribe to our updates or provide contact information. By subscribing to our news updates, you consent to receiving editorial content in your designated inbox. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every email communication.</p>

  <h2>3. Third-Party Disclosures and Data Retention</h2>

  <p><strong>Data Minimization:</strong> We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your email address with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated marketing networks.</p>

  <p><strong>Data Processors:</strong> Your email address may be securely transferred to and stored by a cloud-based email distribution vendor. That vendor operates under a strict data processing agreement and is contractually restricted from using your data for any purpose other than delivering our instructions.</p>

  <p><strong>Retention Period:</strong> Personal information is retained only for the duration of your active subscription lifecycle. Upon unsubscribing, your information is permanently purged or anonymized within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law.</p>

  <p><strong>Cookies and Analytics:</strong> We may use limited first-party technical cookies to improve site performance, ensure stability, and maintain secure operation. We do not use cross-site behavioral tracking for advertising purposes.</p>

  <h2>4. Data Subject Rights</h2>

  <p>Under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR and CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy compliance desk at privacy@yourdomain.com.</p>

  <p>We aim to respond to valid requests in a timely manner and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.</p>

  <h2>5. Security</h2>

  <p>We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of data transmission over the Internet is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.</p>

  <h2>6. Changes to This Policy</h2>

  <p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.</p>

  <h2>7. Contact</h2>

  <p>For questions about this policy or to submit a privacy request, contact:</p>
  <p>Email: privacy@yourdomain.com</p>
